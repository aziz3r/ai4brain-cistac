AI4BRAIN ↗

Five short games. A probabilistic model of the player. A coach that explains itself.

A learning tool, not a medical or psychological assessment.

Why

Honest ranges, plain reasons, private by design

  • Memory · Focus · Flexibility · Speed · Reasoning
  • Every estimate comes with a range that narrows with practice
  • Every recommendation comes with a reason in everyday words
  • No camera, no microphone, no name, no email
Under the hood

Bayesian update of each skill

Prior: θ ~ N(μ, σ² + Q)  ·  Observation: y = raw · speed + 10·log₂(difficulty)
Gain: K = P / (P + R)  ·  μ′ = μ + K (y − μ)  ·  σ′² = (1 − K) P
Noise: R grows early on and with difficulty; lapses beyond 3σ are damped
Collaboration score = 0.20 C + 0.25 V + 0.20 I + 0.20 A + 0.15 R
